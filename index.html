About

To me, technology is more than a means of solving problems; it's an experience that shapes how we interact with and understand the world around us. I'm fascinated by how something as simple as an idea can evolve into something that fundamentally changes someone's everyday experience.

I'm currently studying CS and Data Science at UC Berkeley, where I'm exploring my interests in AI, cybersecurity, and entrepreneurship.

Much of what inspires me comes from everyday interactions with people and the different ways we experience the same world. I find myself drawn to the problems that often go unnoticed and wondering how technology could change those experiences.

I believe the most meaningful technology is built not just around what is possible, but around what makes the human experience better.
